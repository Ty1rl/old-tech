ROUND O AUDIO — edit audio/combat/palette.tres in Godot's Inspector.
CombatAudioLibrary contains CombatAudioCue Resources: stream, gain, priority,
cooldown, base pitch, responsive/loop flags and the existing visual cue name.
Changing one entry changes that sound without editing a controller. Runtime never
modifies shared streams/resources. tools/audio/bake_palette.py reproduces the SFX.

PUBLIC CONTRACT (existing CombatFeedback owner; no second audio manager)
cue(kind, world_point, strength=1) and step(...) remain stable.
enemy_cue(enemy_kind, event, world_point, strength=1) accepts warning/attack/hurt/death.
set_music_playing(bool) opts into the single ambient slot; default is OFF.

CUES / PALETTE
ring_launch / ring_loop / cable_cut / hit / ring_return: spring, spinning metal,
  severed wire snap, dull contact, returning latch.
flash_fire / flash_charged / pierce_hit / flash_ready / charge_loop: shutter thump,
  capacitor launch, sharp penetrating contact, ready latch, rising charge.
  Existing flash_fire + flash_pierce in one update becomes ONE charged launch.
  link_cut maps to cable_cut; valve_b to flash_ready.
cone_loop / cone_pulse / vent: air motor, low puff, broken armor/steam release.
repair_step / repair_full: short part latch versus ascending completed assembly.
room_open: low door slide/chime. player_hurt: low warm impact. ui_click: tiny latch.
recovery_confirm: rising stable chord, separate from repair and cut sounds.
clock_loop: ticked motor winds down/up. scrub_older / scrub_newer: distinct low/high
  rotating textures; sign selects direction, speed changes pitch and gain smoothly.

ENEMIES (each has warning, attack, hurt, death; per-kind pitch/cooldown editable)
Cable/spring: blue, loop, zigzag. Glass/valve: yellow, pair. Steam: orange.
Shield/wood/metal: brute, warden, side_guard, front_guard. Relay: target, skirmisher,
ranged. Small pack: swarmer. Low resonant machinery: loop_boss, sequence_boss,
final_boss. Same-kind events coalesce; no per-frame barks or state/pose polling.

TIME / MIX
World weapons, monster voices and music share world_pitch = world_scale^0.45,
with a 0.22s slew, applied ONCE per voice. Native buses have no pitch effect.
Repair/menu/recovery and time motor remain independent; clock slew is 0.30s.
World sources use positioned AudioStreamPlayer3D with 64m falloff; confirmations
have no attenuation/pan. Native pause affects world/music but leaves responsive
players active. Frozen history discards world transients; exit never replays them.
16 native players total: 6 loops + music + 9 transients; two transient slots are
reserved for important cues. Only unheard pending low-priority commands may be
replaced. Foreground cues duck beds. Master hard limiter ceiling is -1 dB.

PROVENANCE
51 original procedural SFX WAVs by this project, baked offline (22.05kHz PCM,
~1MB); no external SFX recordings, voice models or startup waveform generation.

https://captnphantom.itch.io/psx-ambient-pack (free download, no purchase).

Credit: Ambience by CAPTNPHANTOM. Keep the supplied license with game distributions.
